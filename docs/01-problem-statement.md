# FieldOps — Problem Statement

## 1. Project Overview

FieldOps is a proposed web-based field service management platform for small CCTV, networking, and electrical installation businesses.

The platform will help businesses register, organize, and track customer service requests and manage the work orders generated from them.

## 2. Problem Hypothesis

Small technical service businesses may receive customer requests through WhatsApp, phone calls, office visits, social media, email, and website forms.

Without a centralized tracking system, requests may be forgotten, delayed, or handled without clear accountability.

These problems are currently assumptions and have not yet been validated through interviews with real businesses.

## 3. Proposed Solution

FieldOps aims to provide a centralized system where authorized staff can:

- Register customer service requests.
- Review, approve, or decline requests.
- Create work orders for approved requests.
- Assign technicians to work orders.
- Track job progress.
- Record job completion notes.
- Review and close completed work orders.
- View request status and history.

## 4. Target Users

**Administrator / Coordinator**
- Registers and reviews customer requests.
- Approves or declines requests.
- Creates work orders and assigns technicians.
- Reviews and closes completed work orders.

**Technician**
- Views assigned work orders.
- Updates job progress.
- Submits completion notes.

## 5. Primary MVP Success Criterion

Every registered service request must have:

1. A unique reference number.
2. A current status.
3. An accessible history of important changes.

This is a testable software capability. Demonstrating fewer forgotten requests in real businesses will require actual usage and validation.

## 6. MVP Scope

The initial MVP focuses on customer request tracking and the work-order lifecycle.

Features such as inventory management, invoicing, payments, GPS tracking, customer portals, and automatic scheduling are deferred.

## 7. Project Status

Day 1 — Initial requirements discovery completed. Business assumptions remain unvalidated.
